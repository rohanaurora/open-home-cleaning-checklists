# Routine or recurring clean

Maintenance cleaning for a home that is already in generally good condition.

## Kitchen

- [x] Counters, backsplash, sink, and faucet
- [x] Microwave inside and outside
- [x] Appliance exteriors
- [ ] Cabinet and drawer fronts (not included)
- [ ] Inside empty cabinets and drawers (not included) - Empty first.
- [ ] Inside refrigerator (optional or custom scope) - Empty first for move cleaning.
- [ ] Inside oven (optional or custom scope) - Normal-use buildup only.
- [ ] Range hood exterior (not included)
- [ ] Range hood interior or filter detail (optional or custom scope) - Confirm before service.

## Bathrooms

- [x] Toilets, base, and behind
- [x] Tub, shower, sink, faucet, counters, and mirrors
- [ ] Surface scrub of accessible tile and grout (not included) - Permanent stains may remain.
- [ ] Hard-water buildup or heavy soap scum (optional or custom scope) - Cleaning cannot reverse permanent staining or damage.
- [ ] Mold, biohazard, urine, feces, or animal waste (not included) - Use an appropriate specialist.

## Living areas and bedrooms

- [x] Dust reachable surfaces, lamps, frames, and sills
- [x] Vacuum, sweep, mop, and empty trash
- [x] Make beds when fresh linens are left out
- [ ] Baseboards (not included)
- [ ] Door frames (not included)
- [ ] Reachable fans and fixtures (not included) - Use a two-step ladder at most.
- [ ] Move light furniture (not included) - Do not move heavy furniture, appliances, or large rugs.
- [ ] Heavy pet hair (optional or custom scope) - Pet waste is not included.

## Windows and utility areas

- [ ] Up to 10 reachable interior windows (not included) - Interior glass only.
- [ ] Window tracks and blinds (optional or custom scope) - Confirm before service.
- [ ] Inside empty closets (not included) - Empty first.
- [ ] Garage or basement sweeping (not included) - Only when specifically quoted. No hauling or organizing.
- [ ] Laundry and folding (optional or custom scope) - Machine cycles affect how much can be completed.
- [ ] Light wall spot-cleaning (optional or custom scope) - Small safe wipes only. Permanent marks may remain.
- [ ] Full wall washing (not included) - May damage paint. Use an appropriate specialist.
